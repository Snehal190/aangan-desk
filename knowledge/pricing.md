# Aangan Studio — Internal Pricing Guide

> ⚠️ **This guide is indicative only. Pricing varies by site, materials specification, and project complexity. Ranges may be shared as indicative; no firm quote is ever given on the call. All pricing is confirmed by the assigned designer at the consultation.**

## Residential

| Project type | Indicative range |
|---|---|
| Standard specification | ₹1,800 – ₹2,400 per sq ft |
| Premium specification | ₹2,400 – ₹3,500 per sq ft |
| Luxury specification | ₹3,500+ per sq ft |

*Sq ft refers to carpet area of designed space, not built-up area.*

Single room redesign (all-in): ₹3.5 lakh – ₹8 lakh depending on room size and specification.

## Commercial (office / clinic / studio)

| Project type | Indicative range |
|---|---|
| Basic fitout | ₹1,200 – ₹1,800 per sq ft |
| Mid-range office | ₹1,800 – ₹2,800 per sq ft |
| Premium office | ₹2,800 – ₹4,000 per sq ft |

## What affects pricing

- **Site condition**: existing flooring or ceilings that can be retained reduce cost significantly
- **Custom furniture ratio**: more custom = higher cost; more sourced = variable
- **Material brands**: tile brands, fitting brands, wood grade — the spread across tiers is significant
- **Timeline compression**: fast-track execution carries a premium

## What the agent may say (policy updated Oct 2026)

The agent **may share the indicative range** from the tables above when a caller asks, but must never give a firm quote or a single number for their project. Always pair the range with the caveat that the final price is quoted by the designer.

Example: "For a standard specification, homes like yours usually fall in the ₹1,800 to ₹2,400 per sq ft range — but that's only indicative. Your designer will give you the actual quote after seeing the site and your choice of materials. Shall I book that consultation?"

## What the agent must never say

- A single figure or total for the caller's project ("Your 2BHK will cost ₹18 lakh")
- "It'll cost exactly..." or anything that sounds like a commitment
- A range without the caveat that the designer gives the final quote
- A discount or a promise that the price will fit their budget
